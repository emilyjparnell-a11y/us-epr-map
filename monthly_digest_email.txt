Subject: US EPR Packaging Update - Monthly Digest

Here's what changed in the last 30 days:

- Maine: Fees tied to volume/type of packaging. SO not yet selected as of mid-2026; RFP issued June 15, 2026.
- Washington: PRO appointed by Jan 1 2026; registration with Ecology by Mar 1 2026; supply report due May 31 2026; producer participation required by July 1 2026.
- Oregon: Fees by material/recyclability/volume. Feb 2026: federal injunction for NAW members; trial July 13, 2026. DEQ published ~300 non-compliant producers Apr 9, 2026, fines up to $25k/day.
- Maryland: CAA registration by July 1 2026; simplified supply report (2025 data) due May 31 2026; covered materials list by July 1 2027; full plans due July 1 2028.
- California: 25% source reduction and 65% recycling of plastic by 2032; PRO participation by 2027. Permanent regs finalized May 1 2026. Facing state and federal (17 AGs + NAW) legal challenges; no injunction granted as of writing.
- Colorado: Producers began reporting 2025. Mar 2026: lawsuit challenging a second PRO (LPMA) for petroleum/automotive products - not bagging-relevant.
- Minnesota: Limited registration 2025-2026; PRO operations begin 2027-2028; full requirements (reusable/recyclable/compostable) by 2032.
- Illinois: Would require PRO membership with fee modulation favoring recyclable/reusable/PCR content.
- New Jersey: 50% source reduction over 10 years proposed in the prior (S3398) version; not yet confirmed in the reintroduced bills.
- New York: No milestones yet; would need Assembly passage to advance.
- Hawaii: No producer obligations yet.
- North Carolina: No milestones yet.
- Massachusetts: No milestones yet.
- Rhode Island: No milestones yet; all three bills pending committee action.
